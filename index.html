Pilgrim Mechanica 2

Labs: each system on its own page, tuned live. The game itself comes once the labs feel right. Labs with a biome picker launch on that ground (remembered per lab).

Walker labThe procedural walker on streamed dunes: WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, hold left button to fire. Every gait value is a slider.
War band labTechnicals, gun trucks, six-wheelers, the War Rig and a makeshift tank circle the mech and make gun runs; bandit mechs hold off and trade fire. Space dashes out of the tank's marked shells.
Bandit swarm labHundreds of bandits on foot pour in over the dunes and shoot; rocketeers lob slow marked rockets. Mow them down, stomp the close ones, dash through them.
Robot swarm labTerraforming machines: small scrappers leap on and tear at your plating, medium cutters wind up saw lunges, huge four-legged excavators lob marked charges.
Convoy labThe convoy village rolls over the dunes like a train, flattening a road behind it. Walk into crane range to be picked up, walk the deck, park, and stand on a launch pad to be swung back out.
POI labPoints of interest: rock outcrops with scrap caught in them, wreck parts, crashed supply pods, debris fields, dead convoys. Walk up and hold E to strip them for scrap; M shows the overhead map.
Loadout labEquip the walker: legs, hull, arms or hull weapons, power pack and back mount, with rarities and Protocol sockets. B opens the bay; roll loot, fit it, and walk out to shoot the target dummies.
Terrain labThe ground streaming around a moving marker: dunes, rocky shelves and shallows, the borders between them ("Next border"), streaming cost, desert light.
