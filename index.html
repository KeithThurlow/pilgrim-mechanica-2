Pilgrim Mechanica 2

Labs: each system on its own page, tuned live. The game itself comes once the labs feel right.

Walker labThe procedural walker on streamed dunes: WASD to walk, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, hold left button to fire. Every gait value is a slider. War band labRaider pickups and gun trucks circle the mech and make gun runs, bandit mechs hold off and trade fire. The mech takes damage. Bandit swarm labHundreds of bandits on foot pour in over the dunes and shoot; mow them down, stomp the close ones, watch their nerve break. Terrain labThe dune field streaming around a moving marker (the convoy stand-in): dune shape, streaming cost, desert light.
