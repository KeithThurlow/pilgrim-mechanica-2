Pick a weapon in the panel · Play: left click swings or fires at the dummies, Tab locks one · P pauses the sword and poses it: pick a move and a key, then G moves the hand (arrows), T rotates the blade (rings), E bends the elbow (the orange dot) (drag to orbit, scroll to zoom) · arrows: blue forward, green up (the back of each arm bone, the blade spine), red strip the cutting edge, white ball the tip · [ and ] step key to key, Ctrl+Z undoes · Copy pose puts the JSON on the clipboard
